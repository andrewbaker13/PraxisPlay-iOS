Praxis Play — Privacy Policy, Account Deletion & Support

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

Praxis Play (App / Package ID: com.mktpraxis.praxisplay) is the official mobile classroom companion and simulation controller developed by Dr. Andrew Baker (MktPraxis.com) for higher-education marketing analytics instruction, 4-desk agency pitch simulations, and standalone student practice on iOS, iPadOS, and Android.

1. Information We Collect

  • Solo Practice Arcade Mode: You may use all 10 Solo Practice Arcade simulations completely offline without creating an account or providing any personal information. High scores and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Live Classroom Sessions (Guest or Student Account): When joining a live instructor-led session via a 4-character Room Code or QR code, the app transmits your chosen display name and in-game decisions to the MktPraxis classroom server (drbaker-backend.onrender.com) solely to calculate live classroom scores, team standings, and debrief analytics.
  • Optional MktPraxis Account Sign-In: Students and instructors may optionally sign in with their MktPraxis username/email and password (plus optional 2FA verification code) to link classroom participation to enrolled university courses or host live sessions.
  • Camera Permission (QR Code Scanner): The device camera is used exclusively when you tap "Scan Projector QR" to scan a room code displayed on a classroom projector. Camera frames are processed ephemerally on-device and are never recorded, stored, or shared.

2. How We Use Information & Data Sharing

All data transmitted over the network is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/WSS (TLS). Information is used solely to provide educational gameplay, live classroom leaderboards, and course participation records. We do not sell personal data, display third-party advertising, or share user data with third-party marketers.

3. Data Retention & Account Deletion Instructions (`com.mktpraxis.praxisplay`)

Users can delete their MktPraxis account and associated personal data at any time either directly inside the Praxis Play mobile app or via the web:

  • In-App Account Deletion (iOS & Android): Open Praxis Play → go to the Account tab → sign in if not already signed in → tap the red Delete MktPraxis Account button → confirm your password and tap Confirm Account Deletion. Your account is immediately deactivated, all active session tokens are revoked, and your account and personal data are scheduled for permanent deletion after a 21-day recovery window.
  • Web / Email Account Deletion Request (No App Required): If you have uninstalled the app, you may request permanent deletion of your MktPraxis account and associated data at https://www.mktpraxis.com or by emailing info@mktpraxis.com with your username or registered email address.
  • Clearing Local Guest Data: Local guest session history can be cleared at any time without an account under Account → Clear Saved Room History.

4. Support & Contact

For questions, support, or data requests regarding Praxis Play, please visit https://www.mktpraxis.com or email info@mktpraxis.com (andrew.baker@sdsu.edu).
