Praxis Play — Privacy Policy & Support

Effective Date: October 23, 2026

Praxis Play (Bundle ID: com.mktpraxis.praxisplay) is an interactive educational application developed by Dr. Andrew Baker (MktPraxis.com) for higher-education marketing analytics instruction and standalone student practice.

1. Information We Collect

  • Solo Practice Arcade Mode: You may use all 9 Solo Practice Arcade modes completely offline without creating an account or providing any personal information. High scores and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Live Classroom Sessions (Guest or Student Account): When joining a live instructor-led session via a 4-character Room Code or QR code, the app transmits your chosen display name and in-game responses to the MktPraxis classroom server (drbaker-backend.onrender.com) solely to calculate live classroom scores and leaderboards.
  • Optional MktPraxis Student Sign-In: Students enrolled in a MktPraxis course may optionally sign in with their existing MktPraxis username/email and 6-digit email verification code to view enrolled courses and credit classroom participation.
  • Camera Permission: The device camera is used exclusively when you tap "Scan Classroom QR Code" to scan a room code displayed on a classroom projector. Camera video is processed on-device and is never recorded or transmitted.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to provide educational gameplay, live classroom leaderboards, and course participation records. We do not sell personal data, display third-party advertising, or track users across third-party apps or websites.

3. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Local session history can be cleared at any time inside the app under Account & Setup → Clear Recent Classroom Rooms, or by signing out. Students wishing to delete their MktPraxis student account or associated classroom records may do so via MktPraxis.com or by contacting support below.

4. Support & Contact

For questions, support, or data requests regarding Praxis Play, please visit https://www.mktpraxis.com or email andrewbaker13@gmail.com.
